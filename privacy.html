Privacy Policy

Who we are

Coffee Impero sells portable espresso machines in Qatar through coffeeimpero.com. If you have any question about your data, contact us at info@coffeeimpero.com or on WhatsApp at +974 5177 9171.

What we collect

When you place an order or preorder: your name, phone number, area, address and any delivery notes you add.

When you write a review: the name, rating and comment you enter. Reviews are published on the site after we check them.

When you browse: we use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel, which use cookies and similar tools to record things like the pages you visit, your device type and actions such as adding to bag or placing an order. Your bag and language choice are also saved in your own browser.

How we use it

To confirm and deliver your order, contact you about it, handle warranty claims, show reviews, understand how the site is used and measure our advertising. We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Only with the services that run the site and our ads: Cloudflare (hosting), Supabase (our order database), Google and Meta. Each receives only what it needs to do its job.

How long we keep it

We keep order details for as long as we need them for delivery, warranty and our business records. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by contacting us. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, and manage ad preferences in your Google and Meta accounts.

Changes

We handle personal data in line with the laws of the State of Qatar. If we change this policy, we will update it on this page with a new date.
